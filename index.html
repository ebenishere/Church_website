Welcome to IPC
Living Hope Church

It is with great joy and excitement that we welcome you to our online platform, a virtual space where you can view the updates about worship and other events on a regular basis. Whether you are a long‑time member, a first‑time visitor, or someone exploring their spiritual journey, we extend a warm embrace to you.

At IPC Living Hope Church, we aim to provide a place of inspiration, encouragement, and support for all who seek to deepen their relationship with God and with one another.

Explore our website to discover a wealth of resources, from uplifting sermons and thought‑provoking articles to opportunities for fellowship and spiritual nourishment. Join us in worship and engage with the various ministries that resonate with your heart. We are here to serve and support you on your faith journey by meaningful connections with each one of you.

Thank you for being a part of IPC Living Hope Church. Together, let the Holy Spirit help us to continue to grow in faith.

Blessings,

Pr. George Abraham Senior Pastor, IPC Living Hope Church
